# RuangAI — Monetization Strategy & Financial Unit Economics

## 1. Hybrid Business Model Architecture

RuangAI avoids the pitfall of a rigid subscription paywall that stifles adoption. Instead, it utilizes a **Hybrid Flywheel Model**:

$$\text{Free Consultation \& Discovery} \xrightarrow{\text{Aesthetics \& Sizing Trust}} \text{Pay-Per-Render Credits} \xrightarrow{\text{Actionable Shopping Lists}} \text{High-AOV Affiliate Commissions}$$

---

## 2. Revenue Streams

### Stream 1: E-Commerce Affiliate Commissions (Primary Scale Engine)
- **Target Category:** Home & Living, Furniture, Interior Decor, Lighting, Organizers.
- **Average Commission Rate:** 3.5% – 8.0% (Average 5.0% for verified Star/Mall merchants).
- **Average Order Value (AOV) Scenarios in Indonesia:**
  - *Kost Room Makeover (Organizer, bedsheet, desk lamp, rug):* IDR 1,200,000 $\rightarrow$ **IDR 60,000 commission**.
  - *Living Room Makeover (Sofa, coffee table, rug, curtains):* IDR 4,500,000 $\rightarrow$ **IDR 225,000 commission**.
- **Conversion Multiplier:** High-intent visual preview ("Virtual Try-On") converts at 3x to 5x higher rates than passive search ads.

## 3. Unit Economics Model (Per Active User Analysis)

### Blended User Journey (1 Room Project, 2 Renders, 1 Shopee Purchase):
- **User Activity:**
  - 1 Room Scan & Vision Extraction: Rp 12,8
  - 15 Design Consultation Chat Messages (Tier 1 Flash): Rp 24,0 (15 x Rp 1,6)
  - 2 High-Fidelity Photorealistic Renders: Rp 1.000 (2 x Rp 500)
  - Cloud Storage & CDN (3 Images): Rp 15,0
  - **Total Variable Cost to Serve User:** **~Rp 1.051,8 (~$0.066 USD)**
- **User Revenue:**
  - 1 Starter Credit Pack purchased: Rp 9.900 (MDR net: Rp 9.830)
  - 1 Affiliate Purchase (IDR 850,000 basket at 5% commission): Rp 42.500
  - **Total Revenue from User:** **Rp 52.330**
- **Net Contribution Margin per Converting User:**
  $$\text{Net Margin} = \text{Rp } 52.330 - \text{Rp } 1.051,8 = \mathbf{+\text{Rp } 51.278,2 \text{ (98.0\% Contribution Margin)}}$$

Even if only **1 out of 10 users** makes an affiliate purchase, the credit package sales alone cover 100% of the AI inference costs across all 10 users! This confirms outstanding financial sustainability.
