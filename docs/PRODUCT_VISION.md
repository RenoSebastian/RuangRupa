# RuangAI — Product Vision & Strategic Foundation

## 1. Executive Summary

**RuangAI** is an AI-powered interior design and contextual commerce platform designed to democratize interior styling for everyday homeowners, apartment tenants, boarding house (kost) residents, and property owners.

Unlike conventional generative AI tools that generate surreal, fictional Pinterest-style rooms filled with nonexistent furniture, RuangAI closes the gap between **inspiration and execution**:

$$\text{Room Photo} \longrightarrow \text{Spatial Understanding} \longrightarrow \text{Contextual Redesign} \longrightarrow \text{Real Product Matching} \longrightarrow \text{Affiliate Commerce Checkout}$$

Every item suggested in a generated design corresponds to a real, purchasable product available on leading Indonesian e-commerce platforms (Shopee, Tokopedia/TikTok Shop, Lazada, IKEA Indonesia, Informa, Dekoruma). Users can visualize real furniture placed directly into their personal room before committing money to purchase.

---

## 2. Core Market & Problem Statement

### The Problem
1. **High Barrier to Professional Design:** Interior design consultancies in Indonesia typically cost upwards of IDR 5,000,000 to IDR 50,000,000 just for concept drawings and 3D renders. Ordinary consumers cannot justify this expense for a bedroom, kos-kosan, or living room makeover.
2. **Imagination & Sizing Friction:** Consumers browsing e-commerce platforms struggle to imagine how a sofa, rug, or floor lamp will actually look in their specific living space with their wall paint, flooring tile, and lighting.
3. **High Return & Regret Rate:** Buying furniture online often leads to buyer's remorse due to color mismatches, clashing design styles, or oversized furniture crowding small rooms.
4. **Disjointed Inspiration Tools:** Existing AI room generators create generic fantasy pictures with fake objects. Users cannot buy the furniture depicted in the generation.

### The Opportunity (Indonesia & Southeast Asia)
- **Massive E-Commerce GMV:** Home & Living is consistently one of the top three fastest-growing retail categories on Shopee Indonesia, Tokopedia, and TikTok Shop.
- **Urbanization & Small Living Spaces:** Rapid growth of compact urban housing, studio apartments, and modern boarding houses (kost eksklusif) among Gen Z and Millennials who take pride in aesthetic living spaces on limited budgets (e.g., "Makeover Kamar Kost Budget 2 Juta").
- **Affiliate Commerce Boom:** Social commerce and affiliate marketing are primary purchasing drivers in Indonesia. Creators and platforms with high visual trust generate superior conversion rates compared to static banner ads.

## 4. Key Pillars of RuangAI

1. **Intelligent Room Vision:**
   Analyzes room type (Living room, Master bedroom, Kost studio, Home office, Kitchen, Balcony), floor and wall materials, existing natural/artificial lighting direction, and identifies existing items that can either be kept or swapped.
2. **Context-Aware Interior Consultation:**
   Conversational AI persona trained on recognized design taxonomies (Japandi, Scandinavian, Industrial, Minimalist, Tropical Indonesian, Mid-Century Modern, Modern Luxury, Retro 80s, Bohemian). Maintains project memory across iterations.
3. **Realistic Contextual Visualization:**
   Generates photorealistic transformations of the user's actual room while maintaining structural boundaries (doors, windows, pillars, flooring perspectives).
4. **AI Product Discovery & Deep Matching:**
   Extracts visual attributes (color hex, wood finish, fabric texture, style tags, dimensional constraints) from redesigned objects and queries verified product catalogs on Shopee, Tokopedia, and local furniture brands.
5. **Seamless Affiliate Commerce Engine:**
   Generates secure, trackable affiliate URLs with sub-IDs attributed to user projects. When the user taps "Beli di Shopee" or "Beli di Tokopedia", RuangAI earns affiliate commissions on qualifying purchases.
6. **Virtual Try-Before-You-Buy:**
   Enables users to take a product found on a marketplace and see it composited directly into a specific quadrant of their room with realistic lighting and perspective.

## 5. Strategic Long-Term Moats

- **Visual Product Graph:** A growing embedding index mapping real marketplace inventory with spatial design tags, user ratings, and compatibility matrices.
- **Affiliate & Retailer Flywheel:** High-intent visual shoppers drive high conversion rates, unlocking tier-1 commission structures and sponsored placement revenue from major furniture brands.
- **Data Privacy & Trust:** Compliance with Indonesian PDP Law (UU PDP) and zero-trust security ensures homeowners feel safe uploading private photos of their living spaces.
